AETHERIS-Zero: Operator Guide & Overview

bolt 1. What is AETHERIS-Zero?

AETHERIS-Zero is an autonomous Safe Reinforcement Learning (Safe-RL) engine that transforms commercial buildings into Transactive Virtual Power Plants (VPPs). By exploiting the inherent thermal inertia of building concrete and air as a "virtual battery", it pre-cools zones when electricity prices are low and deep-sheds HVAC power during extreme wholesale LMP price spikes—all while mathematically guaranteeing 100% ASHRAE 55 occupant comfort.

domain 3D Digital Twin

The 5 building floors react dynamically to zone temperatures. Cyan (<20.5°C) is cool, Green/Peach is optimal comfort, Red (>24.5°C) is warm. Airflow particle velocity reflects active HVAC fan & chiller power.

shield OSQP CBF Safety Shield

Every action passes through a continuous Control Barrier Function Quadratic Program (CBF-QP) solved in <1.5ms. If a rogue setpoint (e.g. 38°C) is injected, the shield automatically projects it to safe limits.

payments CAISO LMP & Regional ₹ Pricing

Displays live wholesale electricity prices in Indian Rupee (₹/MWh). When high price spikes occur (>₹41,500/MWh), the virtual battery discharges stored coolness to avoid expensive grid draw.
