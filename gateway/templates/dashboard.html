How AETHERIS-Zero Works (Simple Guide)

battery_charging_full 1. What is a "Virtual Thermal Battery"?

Concrete walls, furniture, and indoor air store thermal energy (coolness) just like a lithium-ion battery stores electricity. AETHERIS-Zero uses this natural thermal inertia to store cheap electricity early in the day and turn off power-hungry ACs when electricity is expensive.

savings 2. How does it save 20% to 35% on electricity bills?

Electricity tariffs change throughout the day (e.g. ₹8/kWh at night vs. ₹45/kWh during afternoon peak 2 PM - 6 PM). The AI automatically:

  • Pre-cools the building to ~21°C before 2 PM when electricity is cheap.
  • Sheds AC load during the expensive 2 PM - 6 PM peak window while structural coolness keeps rooms comfortable.

shield_with_heart 3. Why is it 100% Safe (Safety Shield)?

A real-time mathematical shield (OSQP Quadratic Programming) sits between the AI and the building AC equipment. It guarantees:

  • Room temperatures never leave the ASHRAE comfort band (20.0°C to 24.5°C).
  • Air conditioning compressors never short-cycle or burn out.
